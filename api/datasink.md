title: Data Sink Operators
---

Data [sink
operators](https://bonsai-rx.org/docs/articles/operators.html?tabs=quantitative-operators%2Cmulti-sample-operators#sink)
in the <xref:OpenEphys.Onix1> namespace perform actions with side effects
outside the workflow, such as writing data to ONIX hardware or saving files to
disk based upon the data they receive.
